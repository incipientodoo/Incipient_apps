Customer Credit Limit Setup & Exposure Tracking

Automatic Sales Credit Hold State & Delivery Block

When a customer quotation or sales order is confirmed, the system automatically evaluates total credit exposure against the defined limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is customer credit limit calculated?

Credit limit validation checks total outstanding unpaid invoices plus pending un-invoiced sales orders against the configured credit limit on the partner form.

2. What happens when a Sales Order exceeds the credit limit?

The Sales Order automatically moves to 'Credit Hold' status, blocking stock picking/delivery order creation until a manager approves the credit release.

3. Who can release a Sales Order on Credit Hold?

Authorized Sales Managers or Finance Users with credit release permissions can open the Credit Release Wizard and grant an override.

4. Is this app compatible with both Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Yes, Sale Credit Hold & Credit Limit Management is fully compatible with both Community and Enterprise Edition (v19.0).

5. Can I set different credit limits for different customers?

Yes, credit limits are configured individually per customer on their partner profile form under the Invoicing tab, allowing complete flexibility per client relationship.

6. Does credit validation block delivery order processing?

Yes, when a Sales Order is placed on Credit Hold status, stock pickings and delivery orders are automatically prevented from being validated until authorized manager release.

7. How does multi-currency handling work for credit limit evaluation?

The module automatically converts multi-currency Sales Orders into the company main currency using Odoo's real-time FX exchange engine to validate accurately against partner credit limits.

8. Is an audit log maintained for manager credit releases?

Absolutely. Every credit hold trigger and manager override action (including user ID, timestamp, and justification reason) is logged permanently in the document chatter feed.
