INTERFACE SHOWCASE

Step-by-Step Visual Walkthrough

How Sales Order Approval & Discount Control Flow operates in Odoo

Step 01

Standard Discount Application

Create a Quotation and apply a standard discount within the allowed limits.

Step 02

Quote Sent & Customer Response

Once sent to the customer, the quote awaits their decision to either Accept or Reject the offer.

Step 03

Ready To Schedule

After acceptance, internal confirmation moves the status to Ready To Schedule.

Step 04

Sales Order Confirmation

The standard quote completes its flow by becoming a confirmed Sales Order without requiring extra approvals.

Step 05

High Discount - Send For Approval

If a higher discount is applied (e.g., 10%), the system requires the quote to be Sent For Approval.

Step 06

Manager Review

The order is locked in the Waiting for Approval stage, where designated managers can Approve or Deny it.

Step 07

Discount Approved

Once approved, the record moves to the Approved stage and is ready to be sent to the customer.

Step 08

Send Approved Quote

The salesperson can now send the approved high-discount quote to the customer using the Send To Customer button.

Step 09

Quote Delivered

The status updates to Quote Sent, awaiting the customer's final decision on the special offer.

Step 10

Customer Acceptance

Upon customer acceptance, the order is marked as Accepted and is ready to be scheduled.

Step 11

Final Internal Confirmation

Click Confirm to officially lock in the scheduled and accepted order.

Step 12

Sales Order Finalized

The transaction safely lands in the Sales Order stage with full audit logs of the approval process.

Step 13

Payment Terms Access Control

Assign the Payment Terms Readonly permission in User Settings to prevent unauthorized changes to payment structures.

Step 14

Read-Only Enforcement

For users without the permission, the Payment Terms field on the Sales Order remains strictly read-only, preventing manual overrides.

1. Is Sales Order Approval & Discount Control Flow compatible with Odoo Enterprise, Community, and Odoo.sh?
Yes! Sales Order Approval & Discount Control Flow is 100% compatible with Odoo Community Edition, Enterprise Edition, and Odoo.sh cloud deployments without modifying core files.
2. How does the multi-level approval hierarchy work??
You can configure multiple tiers of approvers based on the discount percentage or total order amount. For example, a 10% discount might require a manager's approval, while a 20% discount requires a director's approval.
3. Can I set different discount limits for different sales teams or users??
Yes, you can define maximum allowable discounts per user role or globally, ensuring that junior sales staff have lower limits than senior sales managers.
4. Are approvers notified automatically when an order needs authorization?
Absolutely. The system automatically sends email notifications and internal Odoo alerts to the designated approvers the moment a sales order exceeds the configured thresholds.
5. What happens if a sales order is rejected by an approver?
If rejected, the sales order status is updated, and the salesperson is notified. The salesperson can then revise the order and re-submit it for approval, with the entire history logged in the chatter.
6. Does this module require installing third-party Python packages?
None at all. It is a clean, native Odoo app that installs in seconds without installing external Python dependencies.
7. Is it possible for top-level management to bypass the approval process?
Yes, the module includes bypass capabilities that can be granted to specific administrative or executive roles, allowing them to confirm orders directly when necessary.
