Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this app compatible with both Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Yes, Customize Fiscal Year & Quarters is fully compatible with both Odoo Community and Enterprise Edition (v19.0).

2. How does the module handle non-calendar fiscal year start dates?

The module respects your company fiscal year start settings (e.g. April 1st to March 31st), dynamically shifting Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 date ranges to match your financial year.

3. Which views support Fiscal Quarter date filtering and grouping?

All standard search views, list views, kanban views, graph views, and pivot reporting tables support the new quarter filters and group-by options.

4. Does it support multi-company setups?

Yes, each company can have its own fiscal year configuration, and date filters adapt according to the currently active user company context.

5. Can I configure custom quarter date boundaries?

Yes, custom quarter start and end date ranges can be defined per fiscal year under Accounting / Invoicing Settings.

6. Will this affect existing accounting journal entries or past transactions?

No, the module non-destructively calculates fiscal year and quarter date ranges for filtering and pivot reporting without altering posted move lines.

7. Does this module support Excel and PDF export filtering by quarter?

Yes, all filtered pivot views and financial reports maintain quarter grouping when exported to XLSX spreadsheets or PDF reports.

8. Is any database migration required after installation?

No, installation is instant and zero data migration is needed. The quarter calculation engine automatically applies to existing records upon module activation.
