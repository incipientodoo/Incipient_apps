INTERFACE SHOWCASE

Step-by-Step Visual Walkthrough

How Recurring Purchase Orders from Blanket Agreements operates in Odoo

Step 01

Navigate to Purchase Settings

Open Odoo Configuration and navigate to Purchase / Configuration / Settings.

Access the purchase configuration settings to verify purchase agreement features are enabled.

Step 02

Enable Purchase Agreements

Under the Orders section in Purchase settings, ensure the "Purchase Agreements" checkbox is active.

This unlocks Blanket Orders and Purchase Requisitions across your procurement workflows.

Step 03

Open Purchase Agreements

Navigate to Purchase / Orders / Purchase Agreements.

Create or select an existing Blanket Agreement with your vendor, items, and agreement validity dates.

Step 04

Open Recurring PO Schedule Wizard

Click the "Generate Recurring Schedule" button on the Blanket Agreement form.

Configure the Schedule Start Date, End Date, and Recurrence Frequency (Days, Weeks, Months).

Step 05

Generate Release Schedule

Click "Generate Schedule" inside the wizard to automatically create the release lines.

A real-time notification confirms how many scheduled PO release lines have been successfully generated.

Step 06

Review Scheduled PO Releases

Access the new "Scheduled PO Releases" tab on the Blanket Agreement.

Inspect all scheduled delivery dates, status badges, and manual release action buttons in one consolidated view.

Step 07

Trigger On-Demand PO Release

Click the "Generate PO Now" button next to any scheduled line to create the Purchase Order immediately.

Provides full flexibility to pull releases forward whenever urgent supplier replenishment is required.

Step 08

Verify PO Creation & Status Update

The line status updates to "PO Created" with the direct Purchase Order link displayed.

A complete audit entry is simultaneously logged in the agreement chatter with the PO number and release date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand or collapse details

1. How does the automated daily cron generate purchase orders?
The module installs an automated scheduled action (Cron) that runs daily. It checks for all draft scheduled lines where the scheduled date is less than or equal to today, and automatically generates purchase orders with all product lines and quantities from the Blanket Agreement.
2. Can I generate a purchase order manually before the scheduled date arrives?
Yes! Each scheduled line features a "Generate PO Now" button. You can click it at any time to create the purchase order immediately. Once created, the button disappears and the status turns to "PO Created".
3. Which recurrence intervals are supported by the schedule wizard?
The wizard supports recurrence in Days, Weeks, or Months. You can specify custom numbers such as "Every 1 Month", "Every 2 Weeks", or "Every 15 Days".
4. Does the generated PO inherit product prices and quantities from the Blanket Agreement?
Yes. The generated PO automatically copies all product lines, units of measure, ordered quantities, and contracted unit prices defined in the Blanket Agreement.
5. Are generated purchase orders linked back to the Blanket Agreement?
Yes. The requisition_id is directly linked to the PO, the origin field references the agreement and scheduled date, and each line in the Scheduled Releases tab directly displays the generated PO.
6. What happens if the Blanket Agreement is cancelled or closed?
The daily automated cron ignores agreements in cancelled, closed, or done states. Furthermore, the "Generate Recurring Schedule" button is automatically hidden for closed or cancelled agreements.
7. Does the module support multi-company setups?
Yes. The scheduled lines, agreement, and generated purchase orders inherit and respect the specific company ID and company currency of the Blanket Agreement.
8. Is this module compatible with Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise?
Yes! It is fully tested and 100% compatible with Odoo 19.0 Community Edition, Enterprise Edition, and Odoo.sh deployments.
9. Does this module require installing any third-party Python packages?
No external Python libraries are required. It relies strictly on standard Odoo ORM methods and Python's built-in dateutil.
