INTERFACE SHOWCASE

Complete Operational Workflow & Use Cases

End-to-end visual breakdown of Lead Bucketing, Qualification Metrics & Neglected Lead Tracking

Step 01

Lead Generation & Automated Timestamp Logging

When a new lead is created in Odoo CRM, the module captures creation timestamp (`create_date`) as baseline.

The system initializes tracking parameters for lead age, expected closing dates, and activity logs.

Step 02

Dynamic Lead Age & Forecast Bucketing

The background cron computes total lead age and classifies deals into `< 15`, `< 30`, `< 60`, `< 90`, or `> 90 Days` buckets.

Forecast closing timeframe buckets are updated automatically to provide clear pipeline maturity metrics.

Step 03

Qualification Cycle Time Calculation

When a lead is converted into an Opportunity, the module computes exact duration in days (`cycle_time_to_qualify`).

Sales Managers can evaluate lead qualification velocity across different sales channels and representatives.

Step 04

Last & Next Sales Activity Step Tracking

The CRM form displays `Last Sales Step` (most recent completed activity) and `Next Sales Step` (planned deadline).

Provides sales reps and team leads with immediate visibility into current deal progression status.

Step 05

Automated Neglected Lead Status Detection

The daily scheduled cron (`_cron_update_neglected_status`) checks for deals inactive for 10+ days.

If no recent activity or future scheduled step exists, `Neglected Status` is set to `True` for manager intervention.

Step 06

CRM Pipeline Reporting & Group-By Filters

Filter pipeline views by Neglected Status, Lead Age Buckets, or Forecast Closing Buckets.

Enables sales leaders to reassign stagnant deals, optimize sales follow-ups, and accelerate pipeline velocity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand or collapse details

1. How are Lead Age Buckets calculated in Incipient CRM Lead Analytics?
Lead Age Buckets compute the total days between today's date and the lead's `create_date`, automatically placing records into < 15, < 30, < 60, < 90, or > 90 Days tiers.
2. What triggers the Neglected Lead Status flag?
A lead is flagged as `Neglected Status = True` if it is active, not won, and has had no completed activity or planned next step activity for more than 10 days.
3. How is Cycle Time to Qualify calculated?
Cycle Time to Qualify measures the exact number of days between lead creation (`create_date`) and opportunity conversion (`date_conversion`).
4. Does the module run background cron jobs automatically?
Yes. The module includes two automated daily cron jobs (`crm_update_buckets_cron` and `crm_neglected_status_cron`) that update analytics buckets and neglected flags automatically.
5. How does Last Sales Step tracking work?
It inspects internal chatter activity messages for the lead, automatically retrieving the name and date of the most recently completed sales activity type.
6. What validation rule is enforced on expected closing dates?
An ORM python constraint prevents users from setting an expected closing date (`date_deadline`) earlier than the lead's creation date.
7. Can I filter CRM Kanban views by Neglected Status?
Yes! Search view extensions allow sales teams to filter or group pipeline opportunities by Neglected Status and Lead Age Buckets in Kanban and List views.
8. How does Forecast Timeframe Bucketing improve pipeline closing predictability?
Forecast Timeframe Buckets calculate the delta between lead creation and expected closing date, automatically grouping opportunities into timeframe brackets (< 15, < 30, < 60, < 90, > 90 Days) so sales leaders can evaluate deal maturity and prevent pipeline stagnation.
9. Does the module slow down CRM database performance?
No. All bucket calculations and neglected flags are computed during off-peak scheduled daily crons or optimized field dependencies, ensuring zero performance lag on daily CRM usage.
10. How does Next Sales Step tracking help sales reps prioritize daily follow-ups?
Next Sales Step dynamically surfaces the upcoming scheduled activity type and deadline from open planned activities, allowing representatives to focus on urgent deal touchpoints without manually searching Chatter history.
