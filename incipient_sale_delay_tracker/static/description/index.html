Complete Operational Workflow & Use Cases

End-to-end visual breakdown of Commitment Date Intercepts, Wizard Prompts & Chatter Logging

Step 01

Sales Quotation Creation & Product Selection

Create a standard Sales Quotation in Odoo, select the customer, and add order line items.

Builds the initial sales agreement before evaluating delivery timelines.

Step 02

Past Commitment Date Configuration

Navigate to the 'Other Info' tab and specify a commitment or delivery date earlier than today.

Configures an overdue commitment scenario to trigger the delay evaluation engine upon confirmation.

Step 03

Automated Intercept & Reason for Delay Wizard

Clicking 'Confirm' automatically interrupts standard order processing when the commitment date is past due.

An interactive popup window (`Reason for Delay`) displays instantly, requiring mandatory operational input.

Step 04

Rationale Entry & Wizard Submission

The sales representative types explicit delay notes (e.g. "Supplier material delivery delayed").

Clicking 'Confirm' validates the required rationale and resumes order confirmation.

Ensures complete auditability for sales managers and operational fulfillment teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand or collapse details

1. How does the module detect when a sales order confirmation is delayed?
When the user clicks 'Confirm' on a sales quotation, the module compares the quotation's commitment date against the current date. If commitment date is past due, the wizard opens automatically.
2. Is entering a delay reason mandatory when commitment date is past due?
Yes. The `reason_for_delay` field on the popup wizard is required (`required=True`), preventing users from confirming overdue commitments without justification.
3. Where is the captured delay reason saved on the sales order?
The delay reason is saved into a custom `reason_for_delay` character field on the sales order form and logged to the order Chatter.
4. Does the wizard block orders if commitment date is in the future?
No. If the commitment date is today or in the future, standard order confirmation proceeds instantly without opening the wizard popup.
5. Can sales managers view and filter orders by delay reasons?
Yes! Sales managers can search, group, and filter sales orders using the Reason for Delay field in standard list and pivot views.
6. Is chatter field tracking enabled for the delay reason field?
Yes. The `reason_for_delay` field is configured with `tracking=True`, ensuring any changes or additions generate a chatter notification.
7. Does this module modify standard Odoo delivery order pickings?
No. It seamlessly extends `sale.order` confirmation workflow without modifying core inventory picking models.
8. Can sales managers require delay reasons for quotes updated after initial confirmation?
Yes. Any time a sales order commitment date is modified to a past date or re-confirmed past due, the intercept wizard re-evaluates the commitment status and mandates updated rationale entry.
9. How does chatter tracking safeguard sales delay records from accidental deletion?
The `reason_for_delay` field is configured with native chatter audit tracking (`tracking=True`), creating an immutable history log in the order Chatter feed that records who entered the delay rationale and when.
10. How does mandatory delay rationale entry improve supply chain transparency?
By requiring sales representatives to explicitly document why an order is past commitment date before confirmation, operations and procurement teams gain immediate visibility into vendor delays, stockouts, or fulfillment bottlenecks.
