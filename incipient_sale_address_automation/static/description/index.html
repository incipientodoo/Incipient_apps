INTERFACE SHOWCASE

Step-by-Step Visual Walkthrough

How Billing & Shipping Address Automation operates in Odoo

Step 01

Create Customer Profile

Begin by creating the main company contact in Odoo.

This serves as the parent profile for all specific addresses (Billing, Shipping, etc.).

Step 02

Add Invoice Address

Create a specific Invoice address under the main company.

This ensures invoices are routed to the accounting department or correct billing entity.

Step 03

Set Primary Accounting Contact

Designate the Invoice address as the Primary Accounting Contact in the Invoicing tab.

This setting guarantees financial communications are sent directly to this specific contact.

Step 04

Add Delivery Address

Create a separate Delivery address under the company profile.

Essential for ensuring physical goods are shipped to the correct warehouse or office.

Step 05

Create Opportunity to Quotation

From the CRM Pipeline, create a new Opportunity for the customer and click "New Quotation".

The module automatically kicks in to synchronize all relevant addresses seamlessly.

Step 06

Automated Address Synchronization

The newly created Sales Order instantly pulls the correct Invoice and Delivery addresses.

Zero manual entry required—preventing costly shipping and invoicing mistakes.

Step 07

Confirm and Invoice

Confirm the Sales Order and generate the Customer Invoice directly.

The invoice inherits the precise accounting contact configured earlier.

Step 08

Automated Invoice Communication

When sending the invoice, Odoo automatically targets the Primary Accounting Contact.

This bypasses generic company emails to ensure prompt payment processing.

Step 09

Missing Accounting Email Scenario

What happens if the specific Invoice contact doesn't have an email address configured?

Our module includes intelligent fallback mechanisms to prevent communication failures.

Step 10

Intelligent Email Fallback

If the accounting contact lacks an email, the system automatically falls back to the main company email.

Guarantees that your invoices are always delivered successfully, regardless of missing data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand or collapse details

1. Is Billing & Shipping Address Automation compatible with Odoo Enterprise, Community, and Odoo.sh?
Yes! It is 100% compatible with Odoo Community Edition, Enterprise Edition, and Odoo.sh cloud deployments without modifying core files.
2. How does the address automation work?
When you link an active Opportunity to a Sales Order, the module automatically computes and sets the correct invoice and delivery addresses based on the customer's defined address types.
3. Does this affect closed or lost opportunities?
No. The module includes a validation check to ensure automation only triggers for "Active" Opportunities, preventing stale data from closed deals.
4. Does this module require installing third-party Python packages?
None at all. It is a clean, native Odoo app that installs in seconds without installing external Python dependencies.
